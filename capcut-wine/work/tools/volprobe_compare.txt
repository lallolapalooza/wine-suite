Before patch 0031 the Wine row was `serial=43000000 flags=0100008a` (and for a *directory*
handle rather than the drive root, `0100000a`); 0031 moves the flags to `0140008f` in both
paths. The serial is unchanged by design (see STATE.md §49).

The guest's volume GUID is a real per-volume GUID; Wine's is the synthetic
`00000000-0000-0000-0000-0000000000LL` where LL is the drive letter (`'C'` = 0x43), which is
what `mountmgr`'s `get_default_uuid()` produces for a drive with no device behind it. It is
stable, so it is left alone as well.

`err=234` on the guest's `GetVolumePathNameW`/`GetVolumeNameForVolumeMountPointW` lines is a
stale `GetLastError` (both calls returned ok=1; Windows does not clear the last error on
success).
